ReflectFX copies trades from one account onto others, in real time. It works with Xblu, cTrader, MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 accounts, and the copier runs on our servers: your computer can stay off.

Each of your accounts has a role:

- **Source**: its trades are copied. It is a leader for other people, or the source of a copy between your own accounts. Leading is free.
- **Copier**: it receives the trades of a source, sized to its balance. Each account that copies needs a licence, at €10 a month.
