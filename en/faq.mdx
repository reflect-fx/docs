description: "Common questions about ReflectFX."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which accounts can I copy?">
    Xblu, cTrader, MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 accounts: live, demo or a prop firm challenge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I copy between platforms?">
    Yes. A leader on one platform can be copied onto an account on another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to keep my computer on?">
    No. The copier runs on our servers, not on your computer. You check your accounts in the app, from any browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work with prop firms?">
    Yes, with challenge and funded accounts, as long as the firm runs Xblu, cTrader, MT4 or MT5. Check your firm's rules on copy trading first. We are not affiliated with any prop firm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the leader see my account?">
    No. A leader sees your first name and initial, nothing of your accounts. You see their win rate and number of trades, never their balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to open trades when I stop?">
    They stay open. Stopping a copy, deleting a copy or stopping to follow a leader never closes a trade. To close them, use **Close every position on this account** on a copy, **Close all positions** in **Copies**, or close them in your trading platform.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does it cost?">
    Leading is free. Each account that receives copies needs a licence, at €10 a month. See [Licences](/en/licences).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Write to [support@reflect-fx.io](mailto:support@reflect-fx.io).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
