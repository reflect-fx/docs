---
title: "Link an account"
description: "Connect an Xblu, cTrader or MetaTrader account to ReflectFX."
---

Open **Accounts** and click **Link an account**. Nothing is installed on your computer, and you can delete the account at any time.

<Frame>
  ![The window that asks where your account is](/images/en/link-account.jpg)
</Frame>

Under **Where is your account?**, search for your broker or platform. The list holds Xblu, cTrader, MetaTrader 5, MetaTrader 4 and hundreds of brokers.

## Xblu or cTrader

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Xblu or cTrader">
    The sign-in page of the platform opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your Xblu or cTrader login. We never see your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back to ReflectFX">
    The window shows **… is linked**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## MetaTrader 4 or 5

Not sure which MetaTrader you have? Its version is written in the window title of your platform.

<Frame>
  ![The MetaTrader 5 login form](/images/en/link-metatrader.jpg)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your broker, or MetaTrader 5 or MetaTrader 4">
    If your broker is not in the list, choose the platform and type the server name as your broker wrote it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the three details from your broker">
    - **Server**: as your broker wrote it.
    - **Login**: digits only.
    - **Password**: the master password if the account receives copies. The investor password can only read.

    You find them in your broker's welcome email, or in MetaTrader under **File**, **Login to Trade Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Link account">
    ReflectFX waits for the broker to accept the login. You can close the window: the account shows up in **Accounts** as soon as the broker accepts it. If the broker refuses, the page says so.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Once it is linked

The window asks **What do you want to do with it?**

- **Copy a leader**: the account receives trades. See [Follow a leader](/en/copy/follow-a-leader).
- **Lead**: its trades can be copied. See [Your followers](/en/lead/followers).
- **Decide later**: the account stays in **Accounts**, unused.
