**Accounts** lists your accounts by role:

- **Sources**: their trades are copied.
- **Copiers**: they receive copies. The **Licence** column shows whether each one has a licence.
- **Unused**: no copy uses them yet.

Click an account to open its details.

<Frame>
  ![The details of an account](/images/en/account-panel.jpg)
</Frame>

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Balance** | The balance read from the broker. Click **Read again** if it shows **Not read**. |
| **What should this account do?** | On an unused account: choose whether it copies or leads. |
| **Licence** | On a copier: whether it holds a licence. |
| **Follow ID** | On a source or an unused account: the ID others paste to ask to copy it. Click **Share follow ID** to copy a ready-to-send sentence. |
| **Copies onto this account** / **Copies from this account** | The copies it receives or is the source of, with their size and state. |
| **Last 30 days** | Its closed trades over the last 30 days. |
| **Proxy** | cTrader accounts only. See [Trade from your own IP](/en/accounts/proxy). |
| **Role** | **Make it a follower** closes its follow requests and deletes the copies it leads; its followers stop copying it. **Make it a leader** stops and deletes its copies, and its licence, if it has one, goes back to your plan. Open trades stay open. An account leads or copies, not both. |
| **Groups** | The groups the account is in. |

## Groups

A group gathers accounts so you can find them. An account can be in several groups. Click the folder button next to **Link an account** to create, rename or delete groups. Deleting a group also deletes its accounts that are in no other group. Use **All groups** to show one group only.

## Reconnect an account

When a broker sign-in ends, the account shows **Needs reconnecting**. Click **Reconnect** and sign in again to keep copying.

## Delete an account

In the account's details, click **Delete this account**, type the login to confirm and click **Delete account**.

- Its connection to the broker goes, and so does every stopped copy that uses it, your followers' included.
- Copies still running on it must be stopped first.
- Positions at your broker stay open.

This cannot be undone.
