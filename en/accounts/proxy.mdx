---
title: "Trade from your own IP"
description: "Connect a cTrader account through your own proxy."
---

By default, your accounts connect to the broker from our servers. A cTrader account can instead connect through an HTTP proxy you add, so its orders come from the proxy's IP address.

<Note>
  The proxy is offered on cTrader accounts only.
</Note>

## Add a proxy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account">
    In **Accounts**, click the cTrader account. Its details open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the proxy">
    Under **Proxy**, click **Add a proxy** and type it in one of these shapes:

    ```text
    ip:port
    ip:port:username:password
    username:password:ip:port
    hostname:port:username:password
    username:password@ip:port
    ```

    Only HTTP proxies are used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use it for a copy

A copy onto this account goes through the proxy only with **Use this account's own IP** turned on. See [Copy settings](/en/copy/copy-settings).

## Change or remove a proxy

Under **Proxy**, click **Change** to edit it, or **Clear** to remove it. Without a proxy, the account connects directly from our servers.
