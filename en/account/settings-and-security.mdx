Open **Settings** from your name at the bottom of the menu.

<Frame>
  ![The General tab of Settings](/images/en/settings.jpg)
</Frame>

## Name, email and password

Click **Edit** on a line to change it.

- **Email**: type the new address and click **Change email**. We send it a code to confirm.
- **Password**: if you signed up with Google, you have none. Set one to sign in with your email too. Changing it signs out your other devices.

## Two-step verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Click **Turn on** on **Two-step verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the code">
    Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or type the key in, then enter the code it shows and click **Verify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your backup codes">
    Keep them somewhere safe. Each one works once, and they are not shown again. They let you sign in if you lose your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sessions

**Sessions** lists the devices signed in to your account. Click **Sign out** next to a device you do not recognise.

## Delete your account

Under **Delete account**, click **Delete account** to close your ReflectFX account for good.
