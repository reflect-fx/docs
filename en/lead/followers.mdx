Leading is free, for any number of accounts. Followers see your win rate and number of trades, never your balance or trade history. You see their first name and initial.

<Frame>
  ![The Followers page](/images/en/followers.jpg)
</Frame>

## Share your follow ID

Each account you lead from has its own follow ID, listed under **Your follow IDs** in **Followers**. Click the copy button to copy the ID, or **Share** to copy a sentence that tells people how to follow you.

An account that copies a trader is not listed: an account is either a leader or a follower, not both.

**What followers see** shows the figures your followers get.

## Answer requests

Requests show under **Waiting for your answer**. Unanswered requests expire after 7 days.

- **Accept**: the follower can copy the account from now on.
- **Refuse**: the follower will not copy the account. They can ask again after 7 days.

## Remove a follower

Under **Following you**, click **Remove**, then **Remove follower**. They stop copying the account at once. Trades already open on their side stay open.
