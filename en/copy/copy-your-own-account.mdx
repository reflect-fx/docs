---
title: "Copy your own account"
description: "Copy one of your accounts onto your other accounts."
---

You can copy trades between your own accounts without a follow request: the trades you place on one account are copied onto the others.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the window">
    In **Copies**, click **Copy my own account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the source">
    Under **1. Copy from**, tick the account whose trades are copied. There is one source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the accounts to copy onto">
    Under **2. Copy onto**, tick each account that receives the trades, and set its size. Each ticked account becomes a copy, with its own size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the copies">
    Click **Create and start**, or **Create, start later**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![The Copy my own account window, with a source ticked](/images/en/copy-own-account.jpg)
</Frame>

An account that already copies this source, or that would send trades round in a loop, cannot be ticked. The accounts that receive copies need a licence. See [Licences](/en/licences).
