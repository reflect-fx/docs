title: "Journal"
description: "Every position your copies opened, or failed to open."
---

**Journal** lists every position your copies opened, or failed to open, on your follower accounts in the last 90 days.

<Frame>
  ![The Journal page](/images/en/journal.jpg)
</Frame>

Each line shows when the position opened and closed (in UTC), the copy, the P&L, the symbol, the side, the volume and its state.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | The copy is open on your account. |
| **Closed** | The copy is closed. |
| **Not copied** | The trade was not copied. The line says why. |
| **Close not copied** | The leader closed the trade, but the close was refused on your account. Check that account, and close the position there if needed. |
| **Not confirmed** | The broker has not confirmed the copy yet. |

Filter by state with the tabs, by period, by copy or by symbol. Click **Export CSV** to download the list.
