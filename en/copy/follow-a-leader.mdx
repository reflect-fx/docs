---
title: "Follow a leader"
description: "Ask a trader to copy them, then copy their trades."
---

To copy a trader, you send them a follow request. They see your name and accept or refuse.

## Send a follow request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the follow ID">
    Ask the leader for their follow ID: 12 characters, such as `7K2M QX4P 9HTA`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Follow a leader">
    In **Copies**, click **Follow a leader**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the ID">
    Paste it in **Leader's follow ID**, exactly as the leader gave it, and click **Send request**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![The Follow a leader window](/images/en/follow-leader.jpg)
</Frame>

The request shows under **Leaders you follow** as **Waiting**. It ends after 7 days if the leader does not answer.

## Request states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | The leader has not answered yet. You can click **Cancel request**. |
| **Following** | The leader accepted. You can copy them. |
| **Refused** | The leader refused. You can ask again after 7 days. |
| **Expired** | The leader did not answer within 7 days. |
| **Ended** | The follow ended. Your copies of this leader stopped. |

You see the leader's win rate, closed trades and the date they started trading, never their balance.

## Copy the leader

Once the request shows **Following**, click **Copy this leader**. Tick the accounts to copy onto, set the size of each one and click **Create and start**, or **Create, start later** to start it yourself later. Each account you tick becomes a copy.

The accounts that receive copies need a licence. See [Licences](/en/licences).

## Stop following

Click **Stop following**. Your copies of this leader stop at once. Positions already open stay open.
