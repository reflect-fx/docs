**Overview** is the first page of the app.

<Frame>
  ![The Overview page](/images/en/overview.jpg)
</Frame>

- **The top line** says how many copies run, and how many things need you.
- **The figures** show copies running, accounts, total balance, P&L, trades closed and licences, over 7 days, 30 days or 1 year.
- **Needs attention** lists what needs you, such as a copy that stopped, with the button to fix it.
- **Cumulative P&L** charts the P&L of your closed trades.
- **Copy links, by source** shows each source and the accounts it is copied onto. Click a line to open the copy.

Use **Link an account** and **New copy** at the top to start.
