---
title: "Performance"
description: "Your results from closed trades."
---

**Performance** measures the closed trades of the selected accounts, copied or not. Open positions are not counted.

- Choose the accounts in the menu at the top, or keep **All accounts**.
- Choose the period: **1D**, **7D**, **30D**, **3M**, **1Y** or **All**.

The page shows the balance now, the P&L, the closed trades, the win rate, and the best and worst day, with charts.

An account's trades are read while a copy runs on it. An account that does not copy yet shows its trades once you start a copy with it.
