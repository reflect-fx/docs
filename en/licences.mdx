Each account that receives copies needs a licence, at €10 a month. Leading is free.

<Frame>
  ![The Licences page](/images/en/licences.jpg)
</Frame>

## Get licences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Licences">
    Click **Start your plan** and choose how many licences you need, from 1 to 999.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Pay by card or crypto on the payment page of Suby, our payment provider, then come back to the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign them">
    Under **Accounts and their licences**, click **Assign** on each account that receives copies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Paid by | Renewal |
|---|---|
| Card | Renews by itself every month. |
| Crypto | Not charged automatically: each renewal is emailed to you to pay on the payment page. |

## Change the number of licences

- **Add licences**: the licences you add are charged now for the days left in the period.
- **Remove licences**: the number goes down at the renewal. Nothing is refunded.

## Move a licence

Click **Unassign** on an account. Its copies stop at once, and the licence goes back to your plan to assign to another account.

## Cancel your plan

Click **Cancel plan**. Your accounts keep copying until the end of the paid period, then their copies stop. Nothing more is charged, and the current period is not refunded. A plan paid in crypto does not renew on its own, so there is nothing to cancel.

## Payment method and receipts

Open **Settings**, then **Billing**, to see your payment method. To change your card, email [support@reflect-fx.io](mailto:support@reflect-fx.io). Suby emails a receipt after each payment.
