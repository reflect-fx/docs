## Before you start

You need:

- An Xblu, cTrader, MetaTrader 4 or MetaTrader 5 account to receive the copies.
- The follow ID of the leader you want to copy. Ask the leader for it.
- A card or crypto, to pay for the licence of the account that copies: €10 a month.

## Start copying

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your ReflectFX account">
    Go to [app.reflect-fx.io](https://app.reflect-fx.io) and click **Sign up**. Sign up with your email, or with **Sign up with Google**. With email, enter the code we send you to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    The setup asks **How will you use ReflectFX?** Choose **Copy a leader** and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your trading account">
    Choose your broker or platform and sign in. See [Link an account](/en/accounts/link-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a licence">
    Choose how many licences you need and click **Continue to payment**. Pay by card or crypto on the payment page, then come back to the app. Your account gets the first licence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the leader">
    The setup shows **You're set**. Click **Follow a leader**, paste the leader's follow ID and click **Send request**. See [Follow a leader](/en/copy/follow-a-leader).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the copy">
    Once the leader accepts, open **Copies**, click **Copy this leader** next to the leader, tick your account and click **Create and start**. Copies start on the leader's next trade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To lead instead, choose **Lead** in the setup. You only link an account, and leading is free. See [Your followers](/en/lead/followers).
</Tip>
